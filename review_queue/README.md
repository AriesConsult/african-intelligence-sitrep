This folder contains **unverified** PDF table candidates generated by the [main SitRep ingestion workflow](../.github/workflows/bdbv-sitrep-pipeline.yml). A filename includes the report number and the first 12 characters of the source PDF's SHA-256. This folder is **not** a validated epidemiological dataset.

## Review a particular observation

1. Open the candidate `report_NNN_<12-sha>.json` and verify the full PDF SHA-256 in [`state/registry.json`](../state/registry.json).
2. Obtain the corresponding original PDF from [the source release catalogue](../docs/DATA_RELEASES.md) or a verified official INSP URL; workflow artifacts expire and are not the permanent archive.
3. Check **reporting date versus publication date**, revision/version, original page, table, row and column labels, metric definition, geographical grain, reported value, numerator/denominator and any `ND` / unallocated categories.
4. Compare INRB's **pinned** curated transcription where available, keeping carried-forward, derived and directly PDF-reported values distinct. Agreement of two transcriptions of the same PDF is not independent epidemiological evidence.
5. Record visually reviewed rows in [`reviewed/approved_observations.csv`](../reviewed/approved_observations.csv) using report number, **full** SHA, date, page/table ID, raw cell and metric/scope information plus reviewer and review note. Follow validation rules in `review_approved.py`.
6. Rerun applicable validation and [publication quality gates](../docs/QUALITY_GATES.md). A source-cell verification must not silently overwrite a competing historical series or cause revised cumulative totals to be differenced into incidence.

**Important distinction:** 17 specific original-PDF numerical readings have documented visual transcriptions in a [separate review evidence release](https://github.com/AriesConsult/african-intelligence-sitrep/releases/tag/bdbv-source-verified-readings-42495579497). That is not equivalent to blanket acceptance of all historical rows, or automatically adding those rows to `reviewed/approved_observations.csv`.

## What to do with unresolved evidence

Retain the original source bytes and candidate entry; flag the issue, such as `DATE_ALIGNMENT_REQUIRED`, `DIFFERENT_SOURCE_REVISION`, `AMBIGUOUS_ZONE_ROW`, `INRB_CURATED_ONLY`, `OCR_UNVERIFIED` or `NOT_REPORTED`. If a later SitRep explicitly corrects a value, record *both* source vintages, original pages and the correction scope. Never treat `ND` as zero.

**Current priority:** keep automated discovery and source archiving running; resolve ambiguous source cells and narrative material in separate, versioned reviews as research needs justify.
