## Non-negotiable final gates

1. Every published analyst-facing historical metric must identify its measurement definition, geographic grain, reference period, source version, original PDF cell (or transparently INRB-only source class), revisions and missingness.
2. Every one of 114 cumulative decreases must receive either a source-grounded revision decision, exclusion from incidence calculations with a documented reason, or an explicit *not estimable* classification. Do not convert a decline into a negative daily case count.
3. Every one of 174 ambiguous zone keys must either be structurally separated with verified original row/province identity or expressly excluded from source-cell certified data.
4. Scanned original cells and substantive narrative passages must undergo visual verification before being promoted from OCR to source-verified transcription; record reviewer, date, PDF source hash, page, correction and confidence. The 37 scanned pages cannot be declared text-complete on OCR agreement alone.
5. Narrative source completeness must have a report-by-section audit of original headings and page continuations, with missing sections differentiated from not-reported sections and from INRB-curated paraphrases.
6. A complete final accepted release must include a reproducible acceptance report that quantifies remaining exclusions. **A dataset with well-defined exclusions may be scientifically defensible; a dataset advertised as complete with invisible uncertainties is not.**
